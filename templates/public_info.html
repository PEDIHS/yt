About YT Studio Manager

YT Studio Manager is a web-based YouTube channel operations tool hosted at yt.pedramhs.ir.

It helps authorized channel owners connect their Google/YouTube account, review channel and analytics data, manage videos and publishing workflows, upload content, and perform other YouTube management actions they explicitly request.

How Google access is used

Google OAuth is used only to connect a user's own YouTube channel. Depending on the permissions granted, the application may read channel information and analytics, upload videos, and manage YouTube resources on behalf of the connected user.

Account control

Users remain in control of their Google account and YouTube channel. Google access can be revoked at any time from the user's Google Account permissions page.

For details about data handling, read our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Contact

For support, privacy questions, or data deletion requests: pedramhasannia@gmail.com

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how YT Studio Manager handles information when you connect and use Google/YouTube services.

Information we access

With your Google authorization, the application may access YouTube channel profile information, channel statistics, video and playlist information, comments, upload capabilities, channel-management capabilities, and YouTube Analytics data, depending on the scopes you approve.

How we use Google user data

Google user data is used only to provide the YouTube management functions you request, such as displaying channel information and analytics, uploading content, managing videos or related resources, and maintaining scheduled publishing workflows.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials required to keep a channel connected are stored on the application server in encrypted form. The service may also store channel metadata, analytics cache data, publishing settings, and operational job history needed to provide the service.

Sharing of information

We do not sell Google user data. We do not use Google user data for advertising. Information is not shared with third parties except service infrastructure necessary to operate the application or where disclosure is required by law.

Google API Services User Data Policy

YT Studio Manager's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Application data is retained only as needed to provide the service and maintain connected-channel operations. You can revoke the application's Google access at Google Account permissions. To request deletion of stored application data associated with your connection, email pedramhasannia@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service or applicable requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

Privacy and data requests: pedramhasannia@gmail.com
